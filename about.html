About Us

Discover the story behind Haddazah Preshy Health Foundation

Our Story

Haddazah Preshy Health Foundation was founded on the belief that every woman deserves access to opportunities, education, and support to reach her full potential. Inspired by the struggles and resilience of women in our communities, we created SAVE HER COMMUNITY to be a beacon of hope and change.

What started as a grassroots initiative has grown into a dynamic organization serving hundreds of women across multiple communities. We work with local partners, volunteer advocates, and committed supporters to deliver meaningful programs that create lasting impact.

Today, we continue our mission with renewed passion, knowing that together, we can build a world where women are empowered to lead, earn, and transform their own destinies.

Our Core Principles

Mission

To empower women and girls through education, vocational training, healthcare access, and advocacy, enabling them to achieve economic independence, improve their health, and lead fulfilling lives with dignity and agency.

Vision

A world where women and girls have equal access to opportunities, resources, healthcare, and decision-making power in all spheres of life, free from discrimination, violence, and barriers to their success.

Core Values

  • ✓ Equality: Championing gender equality
  • ✓ Education: Knowledge as power
  • ✓ Economic Independence: Self-sufficiency
  • ✓ Safety: Protection and dignity
  • ✓ Community: Collective strength

Why Women's Empowerment Matters

The Challenge

Millions of women face barriers to education, economic opportunity, healthcare, and safety. Limited access to skills training, financial resources, and support systems perpetuates cycles of poverty and dependency. Women's voices remain underrepresented in decision-making spaces.

Our Solution

By providing education, vocational training, advocacy platforms, and community support, we enable women to break free from limitations, build economic independence, protect themselves and others, and emerge as leaders in their own lives and communities.
