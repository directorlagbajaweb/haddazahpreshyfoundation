Empowering Women, Transforming Communities

At Haddazah Preshy Health Foundation (SAVE HER COMMUNITY), we believe every woman deserves the opportunity to thrive. Through education, advocacy, and support, we're building a future where women lead with confidence and dignity.

Our Mission

To empower women and girls through education, vocational training, and advocacy, enabling them to achieve economic independence and lead fulfilling lives.

Our Vision

A world where women and girls have equal access to opportunities, resources, and decision-making power in all spheres of life.

Our Values

Equality, Education, Economic Independence, Safety, Dignity, and Community – guiding every action we take.
